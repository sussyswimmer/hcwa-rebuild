# Peer sites and patterns worth borrowing

Looked at 29 Oct 2026. The Vietnam peers are the most useful because they serve the same
donors, partners and families HCWA does. The rule: borrow structure and patterns, never
copy their copy, photos or branding.

## Vietnam child-welfare NGOs

### Blue Dragon Children's Foundation (bluedragon.org)
WordPress (Divi child theme), actively maintained, with new logo files from 2025 and
images uploaded through 2026.
- **Donate split into "Give once" and "Give monthly"** right in the header, next to
  "Start a fundraiser".
- **A thin announcement bar** above the header for the current urgent appeal (right now:
  school transport fares). Easy for staff to swap.
- **Work grouped into four plain-language pillars** (respond to crisis, protect children
  and families, strengthen schools and communities, develop national resilience), each a
  page. HCWA's "what we do" can work the same way.
- **Impact numbers sit right next to a Donate button** ("Changing lives and systems").
- **Stories are separate from news.** Stories are about people; news is about events.
- **Footer carries trust:** annual reports, child safeguarding policy in both Vietnamese
  and English as PDFs, charity registration badges.

### Christina Noble Children's Foundation (cncf.org)
- English, Vietnamese and Mongolian versions, switch in the main menu as words, not flags.
- "Finance & Policies" and "Reports" live under Who We Are.
- Bank transfer details per country open in a modal instead of cluttering the page.
- Stories carry a line saying names and identities were changed for child protection.
  HCWA should do the same.
- CNCF lists HCWA in its partner logo strip, linking to the VI homepage. Partners already
  send traffic to HCWA's site, which is one more reason the VI homepage needs a correct
  title.

### VinaCapital Foundation (vinacapitalfoundation.org)
Vietnamese-first: Vietnamese at `/`, English at `/en/`. This is the URL pattern we
recommend for HCWA.
- **"Impact & Reports" in the About menu**, plus a homepage block of six specific,
  sourced numbers with simple icons ("12,273 children received free heart surgery").
  Concrete beats vague: one number, one plain label.
- **Preset donation amounts** on the homepage, then "other ways to give" (CSR, start a
  campaign, volunteer).
- **Programs grouped by theme** in the menu (health, health systems, education).
- Stories and news as two filters of one feed.
- Zalo and Messenger links for Vietnamese users.
- Footer: child protection policy, privacy policy, cookie policy, rating badges.
- One thing NOT to copy: a full-screen language-choice popup on first visit. It blocks
  the page; a visible VI | EN switch does the same job.

### Saigon Children's Charity (saigonchildren.com)
- A rotating set of first-person "Featured stories" from scholarship students and
  teachers.
- Footer lists registration numbers for Vietnam, UK and US, and says the accounts are
  audited and published. International donors look for that line.

## Recent nonprofit rebuilds outside Vietnam

- **The Shewsy (Liverpool youth charity, 2025):** new site shipped together with an
  annual impact report, custom impact infographics and "how to get involved" graphics.
  HCWA's design agency could produce the same trio (see `10-ASSET-REQUEST.md`).
- **CHANCE (Beirut children's cancer charity):** rewrote the site around donor and family
  questions and added answers to common questions on the homepage, which cut repetitive
  enquiries to a volunteer team. Useful for HCWA's small team: an FAQ on Get Involved.
- **Alder Hey Children's Charity (UK):** rebuilt on a theme as a cost-saving base and saw
  more engagement from mobile visitors. A sensible budget model for HCWA too.

## General donor-site practice (2026 guides)

Guides from DesignRush, Bloomerang and Neon One (2026) agree on a few things:
a high-contrast Donate button in the header on every page; impact shown close to the
moment of giving, not only in a PDF; a recurring-giving option; annual reports, audited
accounts and policies linked from the footer; and WCAG accessibility.
