# Visual materials request for the HCWA website

For HCWA's design agency. Each item lists where it is used on the new site, its aspect
ratio and the pixel size to deliver. Codes (A1, A2...) match the labeled placeholder boxes
in the prototype, so you can see exactly where each piece sits.

## General notes

- **No text inside images** (except the social share images in A3). The site is in
  Vietnamese and English, so headlines and buttons are live text on top of or next to
  the image. This also means one image works for both languages.
- **Sizes are the minimum we need.** Larger is fine if the ratio is the same. Deliver at
  the listed size or bigger; we compress and create smaller versions for phones.
- **Formats:** photos as high-quality JPG (sRGB). Illustrations and icons as SVG plus a
  PNG copy. Logos as SVG.
- **Safe area:** keep faces and important details inside the central 80% of each frame,
  because we crop slightly differently for phone and desktop.
- **Children's safety:** please follow HCWA's approval for every photo. For Little Rose
  Warm Shelter we use illustrations only, no photos of residents. For other photos,
  prefer activity shots (hands, backs, groups from a distance) unless HCWA confirms
  consent for an identifiable child.
- **Style:** warm, natural light, real moments, dignity first. No sad or staged poverty
  imagery. Illustrations: simple, warm, a limited palette that matches HCWA's blue and
  sun-yellow.
- **File names:** `A2-hero-desktop.jpg`, `B1-green-bamboo-cover.jpg`, and so on.
