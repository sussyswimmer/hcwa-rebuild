Thrive Together: Advancing Child Resilience in Ho Chi Minh City

Chương trình Cùng Vươn Lên

Summary

A project funded by CapitaLand Vietnam that gives disadvantaged children steady access to education, healthcare and skills development.

It focuses on migrant children, children without legal documents, children with disabilities, children affected by HIV/AIDS and children living in unsafe places.
