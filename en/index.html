What we do

We care for, protect and educate children at risk of early work, abuse, dropping out of school or the effects of HIV/AIDS. We work with families, communities, local government and partner organizations.

  • A6

    Education

    Free classes, scholarships and life skills for children who can't attend school.

  • A6

    Child protection

    Safe shelter, counseling and support for children at risk of abuse or exploitation.

  • A6

    Family & community support

    Family counseling, healthcare and access to social services in the community.

  • A6

    Policy & advocacy

    Working with local government and partners on better policies for children.
