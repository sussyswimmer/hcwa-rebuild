Who we are

A voluntary, non-profit social organization that cares for, protects and educates disadvantaged children in Ho Chi Minh City.

What we work for

Disadvantaged children and young people in Ho Chi Minh City live safe and happy lives.

Our vision

A society where every child is protected, cared for and educated, and has a voice that is heard and respected.

Our mission

As a voluntary, non-profit social organization that takes no state budget, HCWA works for the best interests of children through policy advocacy, capacity building and social services, together with local government, other social organizations and communities.

Our values

  • Equality
  • Children's rights
  • Professionalism
  • Sustainability
  • Commitment

Areas of work

  • Education

    Free classes, scholarships and life skills for children who can't attend school.

  • Child protection

    Safe shelter, counseling and support for children at risk of abuse or exploitation.

  • Family & community support

    Family counseling, healthcare and access to social services in the community.

  • Policy & advocacy

    Working with local government and partners on better policies for children.
