Notes for HCWA

The Little Rose door: closed, in plain daylight, no motif, no address. HCWA's safeguarding lead approves it before anyone outside the review team sees it.

Traces of children's lives (a chalk ô ăn quan board, pencil height marks) stay off until HCWA approves them (05-DESIGN 6.8).
